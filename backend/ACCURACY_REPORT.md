## Compliance Policy

- Every legal/election guidance statement must map to a traceable official source.
- If a claim cannot be mapped to an official statute or government PDF source, output is blocked and user receives the 1950 helpline fallback.
- Law-change questions are blocked when Gazette freshness is older than 24 hours.
