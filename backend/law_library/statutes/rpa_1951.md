# The Representation of the People Act, 1951

## Section 58: Fresh poll in the case of destruction, etc., of ballot boxes

<mark id="rpa-58-1">If at any election, any ballot box used at a polling station is unlawfully taken out of the custody of the presiding officer, or is accidentally or intentionally destroyed, or is lost, the poll at that polling station shall be void.</mark>

## Neighborly Summary

This means if something happens to the voting boxes—like if they are lost or broken—the voting for that specific booth has to happen all over again to make sure every vote counts fairly!
